Knowledge-Informed AI

We develop machine learning methods that combine data with what is already known about a system: physical and chemical laws, cause-and-effect relations and expert knowledge. The aim is models that remain accurate, interpretable and reliable when data are scarce, heterogeneous or distributed across institutions.

The methods are developed and validated together with hospitals, companies and research institutions, with applications in healthcare, life sciences, industry and materials.

Research lines
