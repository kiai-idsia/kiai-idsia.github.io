## Aim

Personalised prevention, diagnosis, monitoring and treatment of
cardiovascular disease require artificial intelligence models that use
multimodal patient data, and genomic data in particular. Privacy and
governance requirements, multiple standards, heterogeneous data formats and
the volume of the data make this integration difficult. NextGen develops tools
that remove these barriers for several cardiovascular use cases, including
secure federated analytics extended to genomic computation, more effective
federated learning over distributed infrastructures, scalable curation of
genomic data and variant prioritisation. The tools are demonstrated in
real-world pilots in a network of five clinical sites. The project contributes
to the European 1+ Million Genomes initiative and to the European Health Data
Space.

## Our contribution

IDSIA contributes to two objectives of the project:

- tools for the prediction, prevention, diagnosis, monitoring and treatment of
  cardiovascular disease from multi-omic and multimodal data;
- better use of genomic data through data integration and workflow tools,
  including federated learning algorithms that overcome the barriers to the
  integration of health data.
