About

Laura Azzimonti leads the Knowledge-Informed AI group and the IDSIA research area AI in Health and Life Sciences. She is also Group Leader at the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, where the group is registered as Machine Learning for Bioinformatics and Personalised Medicine.

Her research develops machine learning methods that integrate data with prior knowledge: physics-informed machine learning, causal AI and probabilistic graphical models, and federated learning. She is principal investigator, or SUPSI principal investigator, of projects funded by Horizon Europe and Innosuisse and of direct research mandates with hospitals and companies.

This work builds on more than fifteen years at the interface of statistics, scientific computing and machine learning. It started with spatial regression with partial differential equation penalisation, which uses physical knowledge to estimate blood flow fields from sparse measurements and was developed during her doctoral work at Politecnico di Milano, including a research stay at EPFL. It continued at IDSIA with hierarchical Bayesian models and probabilistic graphical models, within a project funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation, and with machine learning for personalised medicine in collaboration with hospitals in Switzerland and Italy.
