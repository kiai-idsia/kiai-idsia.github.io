Join us

We welcome students and researchers interested in machine learning grounded in scientific and domain knowledge.

  • Master's and bachelor's theses for students at SUPSI, USI and partner universities, often on data and questions from our clinical and industrial collaborations.
  • PhD and postdoctoral positions, announced on this page and on the IDSIA website.
  • Internships and research visits for master's and PhD students.
